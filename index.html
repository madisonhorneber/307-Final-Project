Ingridients:
  • 4 fillets salmon fillets
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • to taste lemon wedges
Directions:
  1. Preheat your broiler or grill to medium-high heat.
  2. In a bowl, whisk together the brown sugar, olive oil, Dijon mustard, soy sauce, garlic powder, and black pepper until smooth.
  3. Pat the salmon fillets dry with a paper towel.
  4. Place the salmon under the broiler or on the grill for 7 to 10 minutes, until the fish flakes easily with a fork.
  5. Brush any leftover glaze onto the salmon during the last 2 minutes of cooking.
  6. Remove the salmon from heat and let it rest for a minute. Serve with lemon wedges and a simple salad on the side.

Ingridients:
  • 6 bell peppers, tops and cores removed
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey jack
  • to garnish Chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 Tbsp. extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 2 Tbsp. tomato paste
  • 1 lb. ground beef
  • 1.5 cups cooked white or brown rice
  • 1 can (14.5 oz.) diced tomatoes
  • 1.5 tsp. dried oregano
  • to taste Kosher salt
  • to taste Freshly ground black pepper
Directions:
  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add the chopped onion, stirring occasionally until softened (around 7 minutes).
  4. Stir in garlic and the luscious tomato paste. Cook for about a minute until the rich aroma fills your kitchen.
  5. Add ground beef to the skillet, breaking it apart with a wooden spoon, cooking until it’s no longer pink (approximately 6 minutes).
  6. Integrate the cooked rice and diced tomatoes, seasoning with oregano, salt, and pepper. Let this savory concoction simmer for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Arrange the bell peppers cut side up in a baking dish, drizzling with oil.
  8. Spoon the beef mixture into each pepper, packing it in for a hearty meal. Cover the dish with foil.
  9. Bake for about 35 minutes until the peppers are tender and fragrant.
  10. Uncover and sprinkle with cheese, baking for another 10 minutes or until the cheese is bubbly and golden.
  11. Top with parsley before serving.

Ingridients:
  • 1 box refrigerated pie crusts
  • ⅓ cup butter
  • ⅓ cup chopped onion
  • ⅓ cup all purpose flour
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon pepper
  • 1 teaspoon poultry seasoning
  • 1 ¾ cup chicken broth/stock
  • ½ cup milk
  • 2 ½ cups cooked, shredded chicken
  • 2 cups frozen mixed veggies
Directions:
  1. Cook and shred your chicken.
  2. Preheat the oven to 425° F. Press one of the pie crusts into a 9" pie plate and set aside.
  3. In a medium saucepan, over medium heat, melt the butter. Once melted, add in the chopped onion and cook for about 2 minutes, stirring often, until they’re tender.
  4. Whisk in the flour and salt, pepper, & poultry seasoning. It will get thick.
  5. Gradually add in the milk and broth, while stirring, and continue to cook until it gets thick and bubbly.
  6. Stir in the shredded chicken and mixed veggies then remove from the heat.
  7. Pour the mixture into your prepared crust and top with the second crust. Seal the edges and cut several slits into the top.
  8. Bake for 30-40 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown. During the last 15-20 minutes of baking, cover the crust edges with strips of tin foil to prevent them from burning.
  9. Let the pie stand for about 5 minutes before slicing it.
